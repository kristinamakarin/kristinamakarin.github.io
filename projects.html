Brazilian E-Commerce Sales Analysis

SQL and Python analysis of the Olist e-commerce dataset: revenue, customers, delivery performance and reviews. Late deliveries are strongly linked to low ratings (2.27 stars vs. 4.29 on time), 97% of customers bought only once, and delays came in two waves (Nov-Dec 2017 and Feb-Mar 2018) rather than evenly through the year.

Bank Customer Churn Analysis

SQL analysis of 10,000 bank customers to find who is leaving and what it costs. Overall churn is 20.4%, but it is concentrated: Germany (32.4%), ages 45-59 (49.5%) and customers with 3 or more products (83-100%). Churned customers also hold about 25% higher average balances.

  • SQL
  • PostgreSQL

Data Analyst Job Market Analysis

SQL and Python analysis of global job postings to find which skills are most in demand and best paid for Data Analysts. SQL leads in the US (about 51% of postings), followed by Excel (41%), and Data Analyst median pay sits near $100K compared with $140-150K for senior Data Scientist and Engineer roles.
