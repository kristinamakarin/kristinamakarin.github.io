About Me

Hi! I'm Kristina - a Biomedical Engineer turned Data Analyst with a passion for transforming complex, raw data into clear, actionable business insights. My academic foundation in biomedical engineering instilled a rigorous, systematic approach to problem-solving, while my diverse professional background has taught me adaptability, strong communication, and a practical, execution-first mindset. Today, I leverage modern analytical tools—including SQL, Python, advanced Excel, and Power BI—to uncover patterns, evaluate metrics, and support data-driven decisions. As someone who also codes, I use my web development skills (HTML, SCSS, JavaScript, React) to design, build, and publish my own projects from scratch. I thrive at the intersection of structured analysis and clean digital execution, always eager to tackle new data challenges.
